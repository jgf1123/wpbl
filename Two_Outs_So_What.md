# Design Notes

"Two outs, so what?" is a Los Angeles Queens saying to rally their batters, that the inning isn't over even if they're down with two outs. Indeed, once there are 2 outs in MLB, the batting team scores a run just 12% of the time, while WPBL scores twice as often. The motto also reflects the spirit of the league, made up of women who had been told all their lives to play softball instead of baseball and who refused to give up on their dream.
